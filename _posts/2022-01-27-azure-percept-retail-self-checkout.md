Microsoft ran a bootcamp to test [Azure Percept](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/services/azure-percept/),
an IoT device for edge-AI solutions. Our team at Cognizant built a case study for
retail self-checkout: a general fruit-detection model running on the device, using
Custom Vision to train the model and IoT Hub to manage it.

We described the approach in a post on Microsoft's Internet of Things blog.
