Research

Astrophysics research {% include icon.html name="arrow" %}

Before data science I was an astrophysicist. My PhD at the Institute of Physics of Cantabria studied how dust and gas hide the light of Active Galactic Nuclei (supermassive black holes growing at the centres of galaxies), combining X-ray and optical spectra of hundreds of objects.

My first machine-learning work was also in astronomy: a faster, single-pass k-means for classifying large sets of galaxy spectra.
